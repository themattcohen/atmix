# Google Review Response Templates

All templates are IRC 7216 compliant. No response confirms or denies that the reviewer is a client, references specific services rendered, or mentions tax returns or financial details.

---

## Positive Review Templates

---

### P1 — Generic 5-Star (Brief)
**Use When:** Short positive review with no specific details ("Great service! Highly recommend.")
**Tone:** Warm, genuine, brief

> Thank you so much for the kind words — this genuinely made my day! We work hard to make the process as smooth as possible, and it means a lot to hear it comes through. We really appreciate you taking the time. — Vincent

**IRC 7216 Notes:** Does not confirm the reviewer is a client or reference any specific service. "The process" is intentionally vague. "We" avoids a one-on-one confirmation of a preparer-client relationship.

---

### P2 — Positive Mentioning a Specific Service
**Use When:** Reviewer mentions something like "helped with my taxes" or "great with small business accounting"
**Tone:** Warm, professional — careful not to echo the service back

> Thank you for this wonderful review! We're so glad to hear the experience was a good one. Our goal is always to make things feel manageable and clear — hearing that lands well is the best feedback we can get. Thanks for sharing! — The Alpine Tax Team

**IRC 7216 Notes:** Does NOT repeat or confirm the specific service the reviewer mentioned (e.g., does not say "We're glad we could help with your small business taxes"). Uses generic "the experience" framing. Safe even if the reviewer self-discloses a service type.

---

### P3 — Positive Mentioning Vincent by Name
**Use When:** Reviewer calls out "Vincent" specifically ("Vincent was so helpful and patient!")
**Tone:** Personal, warm

> Wow, thank you — this is so kind! I genuinely love what I do, and reviews like this remind me why. I'm really glad things went smoothly. Thanks so much for sharing your experience. — Vincent

**IRC 7216 Notes:** Vincent can respond in first person since the reviewer named him, but still does not confirm the nature of the relationship or services. "Things went smoothly" and "your experience" are intentionally non-specific.

---

### P4 — Positive with Detailed Feedback
**Use When:** Reviewer leaves a thorough, thoughtful review describing their experience in depth
**Tone:** Appreciative, substantive

> Thank you so much for taking the time to write such a thoughtful review — this really means a lot. We put a lot of care into making sure people feel informed and at ease, and it's wonderful to hear that comes through. We're grateful for your trust and for sharing this. — The Alpine Tax Team

**IRC 7216 Notes:** "People feel informed and at ease" describes the approach without confirming a client relationship or specific service type. Does not mirror back any specific detail the reviewer mentioned.

## Negative Review Templates

---

### N1 — Generic Complaint
**Use When:** Reviewer leaves a negative review without much specificity ("Bad experience. Would not recommend.")
**Tone:** Empathetic, calm, non-defensive

> Thank you for sharing your feedback — we're sorry to hear your experience wasn't what you hoped for. We take every concern seriously and would genuinely like to understand what happened. Please reach out to us directly at hello@alpinetax.co so we can make it right. — Vincent

**IRC 7216 Notes:** Does NOT confirm the reviewer is a client ("your experience" not "your service with us"). Does not reference what service was rendered. Invites private resolution without admitting fault or confirming any detail.

---

### N2 — Negative About Pricing
**Use When:** Reviewer complains fees were too high or unexpected ("Way too expensive for what I got.")
**Tone:** Empathetic, transparent about process, non-defensive

> Thank you for the feedback. We strive to be upfront about pricing so there are no surprises, and we're sorry if that expectation wasn't met. We'd welcome the chance to talk through your concerns. Please feel free to reach out at hello@alpinetax.co — we're always willing to listen. — The Alpine Tax Team

**IRC 7216 Notes:** Does not confirm the reviewer was a paying client or reference specific pricing charged. Acknowledges the category of concern (pricing transparency) without confirming any transaction occurred.

---

### N3 — Negative About Communication or Responsiveness
**Use When:** Reviewer says they felt ignored, slow response, or couldn't reach anyone ("Took weeks to hear back. Very unprofessional.")
**Tone:** Empathetic, accountable in tone without confirming specifics

> We're sorry to hear this was your experience — timely, clear communication is something we genuinely care about and hold ourselves to. We'd really appreciate the chance to understand what happened and do better. Please reach out to us at hello@alpinetax.co so we can connect directly. — Vincent

**IRC 7216 Notes:** "Your experience" and "what happened" are intentionally vague — no confirmation of a client relationship or specific interaction. Acknowledges the type of concern without confirming any facts of the reviewer's account.

## Neutral Review Templates

---

### MX1 — Mixed Feedback (3-Star)
**Use When:** Reviewer has both positive and constructive things to say ("Some things were good, but the process felt disorganized.")
**Tone:** Appreciative, open to feedback, professional

> Thank you for taking the time to share this — both the good and the constructive feedback. We're genuinely glad parts of the experience were positive, and we take the rest seriously too. If you're open to it, we'd love to hear more. Please reach out at hello@alpinetax.co. — The Alpine Tax Team

**IRC 7216 Notes:** "Parts of the experience" does not confirm what services were rendered. Invitation to connect offline avoids confirming details in a public forum.

---

### MX2 — Short or Vague Review
**Use When:** Reviewer leaves minimal context ("It was okay." / "Fine.")
**Tone:** Friendly, inviting without being pushy

> Thanks for leaving a review — we appreciate it! If there's anything we could have done better, we're always open to hearing it. Feel free to reach out anytime at hello@alpinetax.co. — Vincent

**IRC 7216 Notes:** Short and non-committal. Does not confirm any relationship or service. Leaves the door open for offline conversation if the reviewer has more to share.

## General IRC 7216 Compliance Reminders

These templates are designed around a core principle: **a tax preparer may not confirm or deny that someone is a client**, even in response to a public review they themselves left.

Phrases to avoid in any public response:
- "Thank you for trusting us with your taxes"
- "We're glad we could prepare your return"
- "As your tax preparer..."
- "We enjoyed working with you this tax season"
- Repeating back any service-specific detail the reviewer mentioned

Safe framing always uses:
- "Your experience" (not "your service with us")
- "The process" (not "your return" or "your filing")
- "If you've had a good experience..." (conditional, not confirmatory)
- Invitations to connect offline for specifics
