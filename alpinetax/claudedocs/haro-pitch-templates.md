## Pitch Best Practices

1. **Respond fast** — within 1–2 hours of the query. Journalists work on tight deadlines.
2. **Lead with expertise, not credentials** — show you know the topic through specific insight, not a list of titles.
3. **Be specific** — generic "tax tips" get ignored. Concrete examples, numbers, and real scenarios get picked.
4. **Keep it short** — 100–150 words max for the pitch body. Journalists skim.
5. **Include a hook** — one surprising fact or counterintuitive insight that makes the journalist want to quote you.
6. **Match the angle** — read the query carefully. Answer exactly what they asked, not what you want to talk about.
7. **Be quotable** — write in a way that can be directly quoted. Short, punchy sentences.
8. **Follow up once** — if no response in 48 hours, one polite follow-up is fine. Then move on.
