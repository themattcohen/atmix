# Google Review Request Email

## Subject Line Options
1. Quick favor — would mean a lot
2. 30 seconds of your time?

## Email Body

Hi [First Name],

I hope you're doing well! I wanted to reach out personally — if you've had a good experience working with Alpine Tax, I'd be so grateful if you'd share it with others.

It only takes about 30 seconds, and it makes a real difference for a small practice like mine.

[GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK]

No pressure at all — I just truly appreciate the kind words when people pass them along.

Thanks so much,
Vincent
Alpine Tax & Consulting

---

## IRC 7216 Compliance Notes
- The email uses "if you've had a good experience" — conditional phrasing that does NOT confirm the recipient is a client or that any specific service was rendered
- No mention of tax returns, filings, or any specific service
- No phrase like "as your tax preparer" or "thank you for trusting us with your taxes"
- The relationship is left entirely implied by the personal outreach, not stated

## Usage Notes
- Send individually (not mass blast) — personalize the [First Name] field
- Best timing: shortly after a completed engagement when the experience is fresh
- Can be sent via email or as a personal text/message for warmer relationships
- Do not include any account details, return info, or service references in the same message
- If someone replies asking about their return or account, respond separately and do not combine with this outreach
