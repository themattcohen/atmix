## Getting Started

### What do I need to have ready for your first consultation?

Have your tax documents ready: W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, mortgage interest statements, property tax records, and any other income or deduction-related paperwork. If you're a business owner, bring a summary of your income and expenses or export from your bookkeeping software. Prior year returns are helpful but not required — we'll let you know if anything else is needed during your intake.

### How do I schedule an appointment?

You can [schedule a consultation](/schedule/) directly through our website. Virtual appointments are available for clients anywhere in the country. If you're unsure where to start, just reach out at contact@alpinetax.co and we'll figure it out together.

### Do you offer virtual tax preparation?

Yes. Alpine Tax & Consulting serves clients nationwide through secure virtual appointments and document uploads via TaxDome, our client portal. The process is straightforward: you upload your documents, we prepare your return and walk you through it, and you sign electronically. No need to drive across town.

### What areas do you serve?

We serve clients throughout Colorado and nationwide through virtual tax preparation. Whether you're in Colorado or across the country, we can work with you.

### Are you accepting new clients?

Yes, Alpine Tax & Consulting is accepting new clients for individual and small business tax preparation. We keep our client list intentional so every client gets direct, personal attention — not a rushed intake and a hand-off to someone else. [Schedule a consultation](/schedule/) to get started.

## Services & Expertise

### What types of tax returns do you prepare?

We prepare federal and state individual returns (Form 1040), small business returns for sole proprietors and single-member LLCs (Schedule C), S-Corp returns (Form 1120-S), and partnership returns (Form 1065). We do not prepare C-Corp returns. If you're not sure which form applies to your situation, we'll help you figure it out.

### Do you help with S-Corp and partnership returns?

Yes. S-Corp and partnership filings are a core part of what we do at Alpine Tax. Beyond preparing the return, we help S-Corp owners understand reasonable compensation requirements, maximize the qualified business income (QBI) deduction, and avoid common mistakes that can create IRS exposure down the road. These returns have real complexity — and that complexity deserves real attention.

### Can you help me decide between an LLC and an S-Corp?

Yes. Entity structure decisions have meaningful tax implications, and the right answer depends on your income level, the nature of your business, and your long-term goals. We offer tax planning consultations specifically for these questions — including mid-year projections that show the actual dollar difference between structures in your specific situation. [View our services](/services/) or [schedule a consultation](/schedule/) to discuss your options.

### What if I received an IRS notice?

Don't ignore it and don't panic. Most IRS notices are informational or relate to a specific discrepancy, and many can be resolved with a clear, documented response. Alpine Tax can help you understand what the notice means, gather the right documentation, and respond accurately and on time. [Reach out to us](/contact/) with a copy of the notice and we'll advise on next steps.

## Process & Timeline

### How long does it take to prepare my return?

Most returns are completed within 7 to 10 business days of receiving your complete documents. During peak tax season (February through mid-April), turnaround may run slightly longer. We'll give you a realistic timeline when you submit your documents and keep you informed if anything changes.

### Can you file an extension for me?

Yes. If you need more time, we can file a federal extension (Form 4868 for individuals, Form 7004 for business returns) by the original deadline. An extension gives you more time to file — it does not extend the time to pay any taxes owed. If you think you'll owe, we'll estimate your liability and help you make a payment to avoid penalties.

### Do you review prior year returns for missed deductions?

Yes, and we recommend it for new clients. It's not uncommon for prior returns to have missed deductions or credits — especially for self-employed individuals and business owners. If we identify an error or missed opportunity, we can prepare an amended return (Form 1040-X) for open tax years, which is generally the prior three years.

### What is the TaxDome client portal and how do I use it?

TaxDome is our secure client portal where you upload your tax documents, sign forms electronically, communicate with our office, and access copies of your filed returns. You'll receive an invitation to create your account when you become a client. The portal is available on desktop and mobile, and it keeps all of your tax records organized in one place. If you have questions about using it, we'll walk you through it.

## Security & Privacy

### How do you protect my personal information?

We take data security seriously. All client documents and communications are handled through TaxDome, which uses bank-level encryption for data storage and transmission. We do not use email to send or receive sensitive tax documents. Your information is never shared with third parties outside of what is required to file your return.

### Is it safe to upload my documents online?

Yes. TaxDome is a professional-grade client portal used by tax practices across the country, built specifically for handling sensitive financial information. Documents you upload are encrypted in transit and at rest. It is significantly more secure than emailing PDFs or dropping off paper documents. If you have specific concerns, we're happy to walk you through how the portal works before you upload anything.
