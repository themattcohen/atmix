For sole proprietors, single-member LLCs, and multi-member LLCs filing as partnerships or disregarded entities.

**Includes:**

---

### S-Corp & Partnership Returns — Starting at $2,000

For S-corporations (Form 1120-S) and partnerships (Form 1065) with pass-through income to shareholders or partners.

**Includes:**
- Preparation of Form 1120-S or Form 1065
- K-1 preparation for all shareholders or partners
- Reasonable compensation analysis for S-Corp owner-employees
- QBI deduction review and optimization
- Officer payroll guidance
- Year-round availability for questions between filings

**Note:** Personal returns for S-Corp and partnership owners are prepared and priced separately.

## Common Questions About Pricing

**Why are your prices higher than the big chains?**

At the national chains, you're often paying for a name and getting whoever is available that day. At Alpine Tax, you work directly with Vinnie on every return — someone who has seen your prior returns, understands your business, and will be the same person you call when you have a question in October. That continuity has real value, especially for small business owners and anyone with a situation that changes year to year.

**What's not included in these prices?**

Audit defense and full representation before the IRS are not included in the base preparation fee and are quoted separately. Ongoing bookkeeping services — monthly or quarterly reconciliation and categorization — are also separate from the bookkeeping review included with business returns. If you need bookkeeping support throughout the year, ask about that during your consultation.

**Do you offer payment plans?**

Yes. For returns with a preparation fee of $1,000 or more, payment plans are available. Details are worked out at engagement — typically a deposit at the start and the balance due at delivery.
