## Services

Whether you're a W-2 employee, a freelancer, or someone juggling multiple income streams, Alpine Tax handles your federal and state returns from start to finish. Every return includes a review of your deductions, credits, and year-over-year changes to make sure nothing gets left on the table.

### Small Business Tax Preparation

Running a business is complicated enough. Alpine Tax works with sole proprietors, single-member LLCs, and multi-member businesses to handle annual filings, bookkeeping reviews, and quarterly estimated taxes — while keeping an eye on the bigger picture.

### S-Corp & Partnership Returns

S-Corps and partnerships come with their own rules, deadlines, and opportunities. Alpine Tax prepares Form 1120-S and Form 1065 filings and goes further — helping S-Corp owners understand reasonable compensation requirements, optimize the QBI deduction, and avoid the costly mistakes that come from treating a pass-through entity like a simple sole proprietorship.

[Learn more about S-Corp & Partnership Returns](/services/scorp-partnership/)
