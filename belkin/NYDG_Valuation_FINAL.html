January 2026 | Confidential

Executive Summary

A third-party valuation dated April 30, 2025 concluded a Fair Market Value of $11.1M for NYDG. However, this assumes an arm's-length transaction with an outside buyer.

Dr. Daniel Belkin generates 43.7% of practice revenue ($5.63M). In an MBO scenario, Belkin should not pay for his own book of business — those patients follow him regardless. The adjusted fair price reflects only what Belkin is actually acquiring.

Background

Subject Company: David Colbert MD, PC d/b/a New York Dermatology Group

Valuation Report: WR Valuation, dated June 25, 2025 (effective April 30, 2025)

Concluded FMV: $11,100,000 (equity value) / $13,070,000 (enterprise value)

Methodology: 50% Guideline Transaction Method + 50% Discounted Cash Flow

Why MBO Valuation Differs from FMV

The original valuation determines what an outside buyer would pay for 100% of the practice. But Dr. Belkin is an insider — the practice's top producer. His patients follow him, not the practice name. In an MBO, Belkin shouldn't pay for revenue he already controls.

Existing Debt

BofA Loan: $2.2M Outstanding ($22K/month payment)

The practice has an existing Bank of America loan with ~$2.2M outstanding. Interest expense ($165K in 2025) is already reflected in the P&L. The debt is a balance sheet item handled in deal structure — either:

  • Option A: Seller pays off debt at closing from proceeds
  • Option B: Buyer assumes debt, purchase price reduced accordingly

Conclusion

Fair MBO Price: $5.75M – $7.25M

The original $11.1M FMV is appropriate for an outside buyer acquiring 100% of the practice. For Dr. Belkin — who already controls 43.7% of revenue — a fair purchase price is approximately $6.56M. Debt treatment is a separate deal structure consideration.
