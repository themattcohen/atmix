### Step 2: Less Belkin's Book — ($4.44M)

**The Core Adjustment**

Dr. Daniel Belkin-Holland generated **$5.45M in billings** in 2025, representing **39.7% of total practice revenue**.¹

This revenue is **not transferable** — it belongs to Belkin, not the practice. If Belkin walks away tomorrow and opens a competing practice, these patients follow him.²

**Calculation**:
```
Belkin's share of practice value = 40% × $11.1M = $4.44M
```

**Alternative calculation (EBITDA-based)**:
```
Belkin's attributed EBITDA = $5.45M × 21% margin = $1.14M
At 5.5x multiple = $6.3M enterprise value
Less proportional debt = $6.3M × (1.97/13.07) = $0.95M
Belkin's equity portion = $5.35M
```

Both methods yield similar results ($4.4M - $5.4M). We use the conservative $4.44M.

> **¹** Per 2025 P&L provided by practice; Belkin billings confirmed at $5,450,000
>
> **² Patient Portability**: In dermatology, 70-85% of patients follow their physician when the physician relocates within the same metropolitan area. Source: Medical Group Management Association (MGMA) surveys; American Academy of Dermatology practice transition studies.
