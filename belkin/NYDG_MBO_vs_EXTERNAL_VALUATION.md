## Scenario A: External Buyer Valuation

An external buyer faces significant risk:
- **40% of revenue depends on Belkin staying**
- Belkin has no obligation to remain post-sale
- If Belkin leaves → practice loses $5.45M revenue overnight
- Remaining practice: ~$8.3M revenue, diminished brand

| Belkin leaves | 30% | $8.28M | $2.48M |
| **Expected Revenue** | | | **$12.09M** |

**Step 2: Normalized EBITDA (External Buyer)**

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|

| **Risk-Adjusted EBITDA** | **$2,492,000** | |

**Step 3: Multiple Selection**

External buyers would apply a **discounted multiple** due to:
- Provider concentration (40% in one person)

## Recommended Deal Structure

### For Belkin as Buyer

**Target Price: $12,000,000 - $13,000,000**

This represents:
- ~25% premium to external buyer FMV
- ~35% discount to full synergy value
- Fair split of value creation

### Proposed Structure

```
Total Consideration: $12,500,000

├── Cash at Closing: $9,500,000
│   └── Source: SBA 7(a) loan + personal funds
│
├── Seller Note: $2,000,000
│   ├── Term: 4 years
│   ├── Rate: 6.5%
│   └── Subordinated to senior debt
│
└── Earnout: Up to $1,000,000
    ├── 50% of EBITDA above $3.0M in Year 1
    └── 50% of EBITDA above $3.2M in Year 2

Debt Payoff: $2,200,000 (from closing proceeds to seller)

Net to Seller: $12,500,000 - $2,200,000 = $10,300,000 (before earnout)
```

### Financing Analysis

| Source | Amount | Terms |
|--------|--------|-------|

| **Total** | **$11,500,000** | |

1. **The practice is worth 40% more to Belkin than to an outsider** due to elimination of key-person risk
