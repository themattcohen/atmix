## Recommended Strategy

### Lead with Option C

**Rationale:**
1. **Psychology**: $10M is a round, impressive number
2. **Legacy**: Validates Colbert's life's work
3. **Consulting**: Keeps him engaged and earning
4. **True Cost**: Only 14% more PV than Option B
5. **Negotiation Room**: Can "concede" to Option B if needed

### Negotiation Script

> *"David, we've looked hard at the numbers. The practice had an exceptional 2025 - $13.7 million in revenue, 27% growth. Your brand and the team you've built deserve recognition.*
>
> *We're prepared to offer $10 million for the practice. That's well above what any outside buyer would pay, and it reflects our belief in what we can build together during the transition.*
>
> *The structure gives you steady income over the next decade, plus a consulting arrangement that keeps you involved as you'd like. And frankly, it lets us manage cash flow while we invest in growth."*

### If He Pushes Back on Terms

**Fall back to:**
- Reduce note term from 10 to 8 years
- Increase annual payments slightly
- Keep 0% interest for 4 years (not 5)

**Hold firm on:**
- Extended amortization (keeps payments low)
- Balloon structure
- Fixed valuation for future tranches

### If He Wants More Cash Up Front

**Counter with:**
- "The down payment reflects your continued involvement. A larger up-front payment would require a lower valuation - closer to $7-8 million."
- Offer to increase Y1-3 payments instead of down payment (preserves PV advantage)
