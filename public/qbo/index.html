QuickBooks Online Connector

Atmix Bookkeeping Connector · operated by All Solutions Consulting

The Atmix Bookkeeping Connector is an internal bookkeeping-automation tool that connects an authorized QuickBooks Online company to help prepare that company’s monthly close. It reads existing accounting records to categorize new bank and credit-card activity, reconciles that activity against statements, and, after a person reviews and approves each entry, records the approved transactions back into QuickBooks Online.

This is an internal tool operated by the bookkeeping practice for its own clients. It is not a self-service consumer application. QuickBooks Online companies are connected by the firm’s authorized administrator, with the account owner’s permission.

What it does

  • Reads posted transactions, register balances, the chart of accounts, and the vendor list for the connected company.
  • Categorizes new bank and credit-card activity from the company’s own history and the firm’s coding guidelines.
  • Reconciles that activity against bank and credit-card statements before anything is written.
  • Records the expense, deposit, transfer, and credit-card entries a person has reviewed and approved. Nothing is written to the books without human approval.
