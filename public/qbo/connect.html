Connect / Reconnect

Atmix Bookkeeping Connector for QuickBooks Online

The Atmix Bookkeeping Connector is an internal tool operated by the bookkeeping practice for its own clients. A QuickBooks Online company is connected by the firm’s authorized administrator through Intuit’s standard OAuth authorization flow, with the account owner’s permission.

To connect or reconnect a company

  1. Email matt@atmix.org to request that your QuickBooks Online company be connected or reconnected.
  2. The firm’s administrator initiates the authorization and, if prompted, you approve access to your QuickBooks Online company on Intuit’s consent screen.
  3. Once authorized, the connector can read your accounting records and, after your bookkeeper reviews and approves entries, record approved transactions back into QuickBooks Online.

Reconnecting follows the same steps. You may need to reconnect if access was revoked or expired. Reconnecting re-authorizes the same access described in the Privacy Policy.
