Disconnect

Atmix Bookkeeping Connector for QuickBooks Online

You can disconnect the Atmix Bookkeeping Connector from your QuickBooks Online company at any time.

From QuickBooks Online

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon) → Apps, then open Connected apps (My Apps).
  3. Find Atmix Bookkeeping Connector and choose Disconnect.

You may also request disconnection at any time by emailing matt@atmix.org, and the firm’s administrator will disconnect the company on your behalf.

What happens when you disconnect

Disconnecting revokes the connector’s access to your QuickBooks Online company and stops all future processing. Stored connection tokens and working copies of your accounting data are deleted as described in the Privacy Policy. Disconnecting does not change or remove any entries that were already recorded in your books at your direction.
