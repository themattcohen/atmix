The average pediatrician in this country sees 2,400 patients. There is no honest way to be the doctor to 2,400 children — you become the processor.

This practice is the opposite. I take 110 families at a time — ten in person, a hundred through the app. I carry one phone. How it works.

The mechanics are the same at every tier: you message me, I respond, prescriptions go to your pharmacy the same day. The tiers change the volume of access, the speed of response, and whether I come to your house. Here is the workflow, tier by tier.

The House — in your kitchen.

You text me. I am at your door that day, usually within two hours. I examine your child in their own bedroom, with their own toys, on their own schedule. Strep swab, ear check, rash eval, fever workup — all performed at your kitchen table. Prescriptions are sent to your pharmacy before I leave. If a specialist referral is needed, I make the call while I am still with you. Between visits, the app is open to your family twenty-four hours a day. I answer.

The Practice — at the speed of a text.

You message me through the Dr. Stacy app. For most things, a photo plus a few sentences is enough — rashes, conjunctivitis, sore throats, minor injuries. I review within an hour during business hours, urgent matters sooner. For anything that needs a closer look, you book a video visit, usually same-day; four are included each month. Straightforward prescriptions are sent to your pharmacy within the hour of the visit. For vaccines, injections, or physical exams that require hands-on care, you see one of my partner pediatric clinics — I coordinate the referral and review the results.

The Roster — when it is needed.

Twenty-five messages per month, two video visits. Same-day response during business hours is guaranteed. Prescriptions are handled the same way — fast, same day for straightforward conditions. After-hours messages route to a named covering pediatrician. For vaccines, physicals, or anything requiring in-person care, the same partner-clinic model applies. This tier is the pediatric equivalent of a good subscription: quiet most of the time, and there when you need it.

What this is not.

The membership replaces your pediatrician, not your insurance. Hospital stays, specialists, imaging, and emergency care all still go through your insurance plan. If you do not have one, this is not the right starting point. I will tell you that directly rather than let you find out later.

I am a telemedicine practice with a house-call option. I do not maintain a physical office. Vaccines, injections, sports physicals, and anything that requires hands-on examination are handled through a partner pediatric clinic — I refer, they perform, I review. Controlled substances have federal telehealth restrictions and are not part of this practice.

Not covered: hospital admission, specialist visits, ED visits, imaging, prescription costs at your pharmacy, vaccines and injectable medications, controlled substances.
