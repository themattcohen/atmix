What full representation looks like

Most sellers hire a listing broker. They get an intro and paperwork. They needed someone in the chair beside them.

Someday does the work most listing services don't — the work that moves the price and the work that protects the seller.

The deal narrative
Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM), positioning, defensible valuation. We write the firm so the buyer sees their growth thesis, not their fixer-upper.
The diligence
Data room prep, response strategy, surfacing risk before the buyer does. Two re-trades caught in diligence pay for the engagement.
The negotiation
LOI, term sheet, definitive documents. Not introductions — advocacy. Sara represents one side of the table only.
The close
Signing day, transition planning, escrow, holdback, earnout structuring. The mechanics of the actual transaction.
The 90 days after
Client retention, staff transition, your identity after the deal. In the engagement, not an upsell.

Sara leads the process. Matt handles the math and operations. We are not a brokerage. We are seller-side M&A counsel for accounting firms.

What you'll experience

What the process looks like.

Written from where you'll be sitting — not from where we'll be sitting.

  1. You book a 15-minute call with Matt.

    Three questions in the form: revenue range, owner count, timeline. Matt sees your answers before the call.

  2. Matt tells you whether your firm is positioned for a deal in the next 1–3 years.

    If it's not, he tells you that too. The call is diagnostic, not promotional. No pitch.

  3. If your timeline is real, Matt introduces you to Sara.

    Sara takes a 45-minute working session — not a sales call. She walks you through what your firm would trade for today and what to fix to widen the band by 2027.

  4. If you decide to work together, Sara represents you end to end.

    CIM in 4–6 weeks. Buyer process begins after. Most deals close 4–8 months from engagement to wire.

  5. We sit through the first 90 days post-close.

    Client transitions, staff retention, your role and identity after handoff. In the engagement, not an upsell.

Questions owners ask

Before you book.

What size firm do you work with?

We work with practices billing $300K and up. Most clients are between $500K and $5M in annual revenue.

How much do you charge?

We work on a flat engagement fee plus a success fee at close. Total fees typically run [X]% of transaction value — competitive with full-service M&A advisory, lower than the all-in cost of a broker plus separate legal counsel. We share the structure on the discovery call.

Do you only work with Colorado firms?

Most of our work is in Colorado, but we represent firms nationally. We're licensed in CO; for out-of-state matters we partner with local counsel as needed.

I'm not sure I want to sell yet. Should I still talk to Matt?

Yes. About half of Matt's calls end with "now is not the right time" — and a plan to revisit in 12 or 24 months. The most common mistake we see is owners deciding to sell six months before they wish they had started preparing.

What happens if I decide not to work with you after the call?

Nothing. The 15-minute call with Matt and the 45-minute working session with Sara are both free. We don't put owners under contract until we've agreed on the engagement scope and you've seen the proposal.
