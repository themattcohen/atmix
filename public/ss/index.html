What full representation looks like

Most sellers hire a listing broker. They get an intro and paperwork. They needed someone in the chair beside them.

Someday Consulting does the work most listing services don't — the work that moves the price and the work that protects the seller.

The deal narrative
Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM), positioning, defensible valuation. Sara writes the firm so the buyer sees their growth thesis, not their fixer-upper.
The diligence
Data room prep, response strategy, surfacing risk before the buyer does. Two re-trades caught in diligence pay for the engagement.
The negotiation
LOI, term sheet, definitive documents. Sara is in every conversation with the buyer's counsel. The negotiation does not get delegated.
The close
Signing day, transition planning, escrow, holdback, earnout structuring. The mechanics of the actual transaction.
The 90 days after
Client retention, staff transition, your identity after the deal. In the engagement, not an upsell.

Sara represents the seller end to end. Matt runs the first call so Sara's time stays on the work. We are not a brokerage. We are seller-side M&A counsel for accounting firms.

What you'll experience

What the process looks like.

Written from where you'll be sitting — not from where we'll be sitting.

  1. You book a 15-minute call with Matt.

    Three questions on the form first: revenue range, owner count, timeline. Matt sees your answers before the call.

  2. Matt tells you whether your firm is positioned for a deal in the next 1–3 years.

    If it's not, he tells you that. The call is diagnostic. No pitch.

  3. If your timeline is real, you work with Sara directly from there.

    Sara takes a 45-minute working session — not a sales call. She walks you through what your firm would trade for today and what would widen the band by 2027.

  4. If you decide to engage, Sara represents you end to end.

    CIM in 4–6 weeks. Buyer process kicks off after. Most deals close 4–8 months from engagement to wire. Sara is in every conversation with the buyer's counsel; the negotiation does not get delegated.

  5. Sara stays through the first 90 days post-close.

    Client transitions, staff retention, your role and identity after handoff. In the engagement, not an upsell.

Questions owners ask

Before you book.

What size firm do you work with?

Sara works with practices billing $300K and up. Most clients are between $500K and $5M in annual revenue.

How much do you charge?

A flat engagement fee plus a success fee at close. Total fees typically run [X]% of transaction value — competitive with full-service M&A advisory, lower than the all-in cost of a broker plus separate legal counsel. Sara shares the structure on the working session.

Do you only work with Colorado firms?

Most of Sara's work is in Colorado, but she represents firms nationally. She's licensed in CO; for out-of-state matters she partners with local counsel as needed.

I'm not sure I want to sell yet. Should I still book the call?

Yes. About half of Matt's calls end with "now is not the right time" — and a plan to revisit in 12 or 24 months. The most common mistake we see is owners deciding to sell six months before they wish they had started preparing.

What happens if I decide not to engage after the working session?

Nothing. Both the 15-minute call with Matt and the 45-minute working session with Sara are free. We don't put owners under contract until you've seen the proposal and agreed on scope.
