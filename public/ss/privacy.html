Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-02 — Someday Consultants, LLC — 191 University Blvd, Ste 183, Denver CO 80206

Who we are

Someday Consultants, LLC operates the website at somedayconsultants.com and several sending domains (somedayhq.com, somedaycpa.com, somedaypartners.com, somedaymna.com, workwithsomeday.com). We provide sell-side M&A advisory services for accounting firm owners. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice.

Questions about this policy: privacy@somedayconsultants.com

Information we collect

We collect information in two ways: directly from you when you submit a form, and automatically from your browser.

Form submissions. When you submit an intake form or request a downloadable resource (positioning chapter or deal post-mortem), we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your work email address
  • For the intake form: your firm's annual revenue range, number of equity owners, and transition timeline

Automatic data. Our infrastructure and analytics tools collect standard technical data when you visit the site:

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and pages viewed (via Cloudflare infrastructure logs and analytics)
  • Session behavior data (via Microsoft Clarity heatmaps and session recording)
  • Advertising and conversion signals (via LinkedIn Insight Tag, Google Analytics 4, and Meta Pixel/Conversions API)

How we use your information

  • To deliver the resource you requested (chapter PDF or post-mortem PDF) by email
  • To schedule and conduct a 15-minute advisory call if you submit the intake form
  • To send a single follow-up email two weeks after a resource download to ask if it was useful
  • To improve the site based on analytics data
  • To measure advertising effectiveness and reach relevant audiences on LinkedIn, Google, and Meta platforms

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Third-party processors

We share data with the following service providers who process it on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare — infrastructure, CDN, bot protection (Turnstile), and basic analytics. Data processed under Cloudflare's DPA.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery of downloadable resources and follow-up messages.
  • Go High Level (GHL) — CRM and calendar scheduling for intake calls. Data stored in GHL's US-based infrastructure.
  • Microsoft Clarity — session recording and heatmap analytics.
  • Google Analytics 4 — site analytics. Data transmitted to Google servers.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag — conversion tracking and audience building for LinkedIn advertising.
  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API — conversion tracking for Meta advertising platforms.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies set by the third-party services listed above. These may include advertising cookies used to build audiences for retargeting. We do not currently display a cookie consent banner. By using this site, you consent to the use of these technologies as described in this policy.

Cold outreach

Someday Consultants conducts B2B cold email outreach as part of its business development. If you received a cold email from us and want to stop receiving future messages, reply to that email with an unsubscribe request. Your address will be suppressed from all future sends within 10 business days.

Data retention

We retain form submission data for as long as it is relevant to an active or prospective advisory engagement, and for up to three years thereafter unless you request deletion sooner. Analytics and infrastructure log data is retained according to each provider's standard retention schedule.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by emailing privacy@somedayconsultants.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are located in a jurisdiction with specific data privacy rights (such as California under CCPA, or the European Economic Area under GDPR), those rights apply to the extent required by applicable law.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
