Atmix Workspace Connector

A self-hosted connector, operated by Atmix (Matt Cohen), that links a Google account you authorize to an AI assistant — such as Anthropic’s Claude — using the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

The Atmix Workspace Connector lets the assistant carry out actions on your own Google data — and only the specific actions you explicitly request. It acts on your account alone, only when you ask, and never runs background crawls or bulk exports of your data.

What it does

  • Read, search, draft, send, label, and organize messages in your Gmail, at your direction.
  • Open, search, create, and modify files in your Google Drive.
  • Read, create, and modify events on your Google Calendar.
  • Read and edit your Google Sheets, Docs, and Slides.
  • Read and manage your Google Contacts, Tasks, and Forms.
  • Work with Google Apps Script projects and Google Chat messages you authorize.

Data, privacy, and Google’s Limited Use policy

When you connect, you review and approve the specific Google permissions on Google’s standard OAuth consent screen. The connector requests access only to your own account.

The Atmix Workspace Connector’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your Google data is used only to fulfill the requests you make. It is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. See the full Privacy Policy for details on what data is accessed, how it is handled, and how it is shared.

Connecting and revoking access

You connect by authorizing the connector through Google’s standard OAuth consent screen. You can review and revoke its access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
