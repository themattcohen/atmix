The standard

Four disciplines. No exceptions, and nothing else claimed.

  1. 01

    Tested every lot

    Each batch is sent to an independent, accredited laboratory for identity and purity before it is offered. A batch that does not pass is never listed.

  2. 02

    Traceable to the batch

    Every vial carries a lot number that resolves to a single published Certificate of Analysis. The record on the vial and the record on file are the same record.

  3. 03

    Limited by design

    Batches are small and discrete. Each is tested as a unit, offered until it is gone, and not restocked. A new batch is released in its place.

  4. 04

    Local, in hand

    No forwarding, no multi-week wait, no exposure on your side. A single person is accountable for what you receive.
