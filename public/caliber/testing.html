Design prototype

Caliber is a design prototype. The products, certificates of analysis, laboratories, and customer reviews shown here are fictional.

Nothing reaches the catalogue until an independent laboratory has confirmed what it is and how pure it is. The process is the same for every batch, without exception.

The process

Five steps. Nothing skipped.

  1. 01

    Source qualification

    Every synthesis source is qualified before a batch is placed. Sourcing is reviewed on an ongoing basis, not assumed once and left alone.

  2. 02

    Independent analysis

    Identity and purity are established by RP-HPLC and mass spectrometry, performed by a laboratory with no stake in the result.

  3. 03

    Contaminant screening

    Qualification includes screening for heavy metals, residual solvents, and endotoxin, in addition to the identity and purity panel.

  4. 04

    Release on a pass, only

    A batch that does not clear every panel is not offered, adjusted, or blended with one that did. It is set aside.

  5. 05

    Published, and tied to the lot

    The resulting Certificate of Analysis is published against the lot number printed on the vial, and stays available for lookup.
