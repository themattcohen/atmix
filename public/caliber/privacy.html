Privacy

Caliber collects the minimum information required to serve a local, research-use customer base. This page describes what is collected, why, and how it is handled. It is not a substitute for counsel.

What is collected

In the ordinary course of reservation and local pickup, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, phone number, and email, used to confirm and coordinate a reservation.
  • A 21+ age acknowledgement, recorded at the point of reservation.
  • Order records — items reserved, lot numbers, dates, and pickup confirmation.

We do not collect payment card data directly; any such data is handled by a third-party processor under its own terms.

How it is used

Information collected is used only to fulfill a reservation, maintain batch and lot records, and respond to a request from the person who provided it. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the transaction it was given for.

Not sold

Caliber does not sell, rent, or trade customer information to any third party.

Retention

Order and lot records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to support traceability and to meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact details are retained no longer than necessary for that purpose.

Requests

To review, correct, or request deletion of information held about you, use the contact channel listed in the footer.
