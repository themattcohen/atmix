1. Executive Summary

This document presents the findings of a comprehensive financial and operational audit of MonsterBass (Outdoor Playground Inc), covering January 2024 through December 2025. The audit integrates data from QuickBooks Online with granular Shopify transaction data (101,126 orders, 19,176 unique customers, 871 payouts) and Shopify Capital loan records.

Bottom line: The books are more functional than initially assessed. COGS and inventory ARE being properly managed via monthly journal entries. Credit cards ARE being reconciled. The core accounting is sound. However, revenue recognition still uses payout-based recording (not GAAP), AP aging shows 48% over 90 days, and the company has negative equity. The path forward requires AP management, revenue recognition correction, and cost optimization.

Cost Reduction Opportunities

FLAG 1: Redundant Content Contractors ($218K/year)
Six to seven separate content/media contractors is excessive for a $1.7M business. Consolidating to two contractors could save $96-120K/year.
FLAG 2: Marketing at 40% of Revenue
Total marketing + creative = $677K on $1.7M revenue = 40%. Target should be 15-20%. Potential savings: $250K+ annually.
FLAG 3: FedEx Shipping ($239K)
USPS Priority Mail or UPS Ground typically 20-40% cheaper for boxes under 5 lbs. A 25% reduction saves $60K.

5. Strategic CFO Insights

Red Flags

1. AP Aging: $229K in accounts payable with 48% ($111K) over 90 days past due. Indicates cash flow stress requiring vendor communication strategy.
Source: OUTDOOR PLAYGROUND_A_P Aging Summary Report.xlsx
2. Negative Equity: ($759,779) total equity. Company is technically insolvent.
Source: outdoor_playground/balance_sheet_monthly.csv
3. Revenue Declining: 12-19% YoY decline. Subscribers churning faster than being replaced.
Source: derived_reports/monthly_revenue_by_channel.csv
4. Cash Position: ~$100K cash with $21-34K/month debt service. Runway: 2-4 months.
Source: outdoor_playground/balance_sheet_monthly.csv, derived_reports/monthly_cash_flow.csv

Green Flags

1. Repeat Rate: 58.2% repeat customer rate vs 25-30% industry average. Product-market fit confirmed.
Source: shopify_analysis/customer_summary.md
2. Subscription Revenue: 71.6% of Shopify revenue from subscription boxes. Predictable, recurring.
Source: shopify_analysis/revenue_by_product_type.csv
3. Low Refunds: 2.4% refund rate vs 5-10% industry average. Customer satisfaction high.
Source: shopify_analysis/refund_summary.md
4. Gross Margin: 39.0% gross margin is reasonable for D2C subscription.
Source: outdoor_playground/general_ledger.csv (COGS calculation)
5. Accounting Controls: COGS, inventory, and credit cards are being properly managed.
Source: outdoor_playground/general_ledger.csv, balance_sheet_monthly.csv
