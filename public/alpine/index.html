Complete Digital Overhaul — Project Plan
Prepared March 2026  |  Confidential

Executive Summary

This plan covers a complete digital transformation for Alpine Tax & Consulting LLC, a Denver-based tax practice serving 150–300 clients (individuals + small businesses, S-corps, partnerships).

What we're building:

  • New WordPress website — 22-25 pages replacing the current 5-page GoDaddy Website Builder site. Professional design, full SEO, blog, scheduling, and client portal integration.
  • Google Business Profile — Created from scratch with proper SAB configuration, photos, and a weekly posting calendar.
  • Local SEO foundation — 75+ citations within 3 months, NAP consistency, service-area pages for 7 Denver metro cities.
  • Review generation — Automated pipeline via TaxDome targeting 120+ Google reviews within 12 months.
  • Content strategy — S-Corp pillar content (primary differentiator), 2 blog posts/month, 12-month editorial calendar.
  • EA transition plan — Same-day credential updates across all channels when Vincent passes the SEE.

Estimated Year 1 cost: $1,500–$2,500 (hosting, tools, memberships).

Design Direction

  • Colors: Mountain blue (#1B4965) primary, teal (#2A9D8F) secondary, terracotta (#E76F51) accent CTAs, off-white (#FAFBFC) backgrounds
  • Typography: DM Sans (headings), Inter (body)
  • Imagery: Professional headshot of Vinnie (required), Colorado mountain/Denver skyline hero, no generic stock photos
  • Layout: White space-forward, card-based service tiles, alternating white/light-gray sections
Credential Language Rules (Critical)
NEVER use: CPA, Certified Public Accountant, licensed accountant
SAFE to use: “tax professional”, “tax preparer”, “IRS-registered tax preparer”, “PTIN holder”
EA progress: “Currently pursuing Enrolled Agent designation” (only if actively taking SEE exams)
Positioning: Lead with EXPERIENCE + PERSONAL SERVICE + ACCESSIBILITY, not credentials.
