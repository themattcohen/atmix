Rules of the path
Five things onboarding never does.
1Guess the accounting method. Null means ask; the month waits for the answer.
2Create an account. A missing park account stops the client and asks.
3Apply an unconfirmed rule silently. Firm-precedent seeds are marked until the owner confirms.
4Sign in anywhere. The Intuit consent is a person's, by hand, every time.
5Push, plug or post during learning. The shadow close writes a diff, never a plan.
