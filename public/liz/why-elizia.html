Controls on every write
Post freely. The controls do the worrying.

01Collision check

Live QuickBooks is compared with the saved baseline before each push. Red, and the push refuses to run.

02Write log

Every post is recorded before it happens, with an undo by record and an audit against QuickBooks' own change feed.

03Fresh reviewer

A second agent with no memory of the work reads the month and files findings. Sign-off waits on each one.

04Zero is not proof

A 0.00 difference triggers the uncleared sweep across all dates and a one-to-one tie of cleared lines to statement rows.

Owner rules, built into the loop
Five things Liz never does.
1Leave a transaction unnamed. Transfers and card payments are booked as transfers.
2Create an account. A missing one stops that client and asks.
3Plug. No balancing or adjusting line to force a difference to zero.
4Add a vendor silently. New names are fuzzy-matched against every vendor on file, then listed for review.
5Send anything to a client or a third party without a per-item approval stamp.
