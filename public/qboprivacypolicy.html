QuickBooks Online Connector — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atmix (“Atmix,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a project operated by Matt Cohen, handles information in connection with the Atmix Bookkeeping Connector for QuickBooks Online (the “Service”). The Service is a bookkeeping-automation tool that connects a QuickBooks Online company you authorize so that it can help prepare that company’s monthly close: it reads existing accounting records to categorize new bank and credit-card activity, reconciles that activity against statements, and, after a person reviews and approves each entry, records the approved transactions back into QuickBooks Online.

Questions about this policy: matt@atmix.org

QuickBooks Online data we access

When you connect a QuickBooks Online company, the Service uses Intuit’s standard OAuth authorization flow to request access to that company’s accounting data. The Service acts only on the company you connect and only to perform the bookkeeping tasks described below. It accesses:

  • Transactions — posted expenses, deposits, transfers, credit-card charges, and their line detail and register balances, so we can reconcile against bank and credit-card statements and avoid creating duplicates.
  • Chart of accounts — your account list, so new activity can be categorized to the correct accounts.
  • Vendors and payees — your vendor list and identifiers, so activity can be matched to the right payee.
  • Company information — basic company and connection identifiers needed to route data to the correct QuickBooks Online company.

The Service writes to QuickBooks Online only after a person has reviewed and approved the entries: it creates the approved expense, deposit, transfer, and credit-card-charge transactions, and creates new vendor records when an approved transaction requires one. Nothing is written to your books automatically without that human approval.

How we use your data

We use this access solely to perform bookkeeping for the company you connect — categorizing new activity from your own historical records and guidelines, reconciling it against statements, and posting the entries you approve. We do not use your QuickBooks Online data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

AI processing

To suggest how a transaction should be categorized, the Service may send transaction details (such as the payee name, amount, and description) to an AI provider that powers the assistant (for example, Anthropic) for processing in real time, and a suggested categorization is returned. The AI provider processes this data as our service provider, under its own terms, only to perform the request — it does not use your data to train its models. A person reviews and approves the categorization before anything is written back to QuickBooks Online.

How we store and protect your data

To keep your company connected, we store the OAuth access and refresh tokens that Intuit issues, encrypted at rest, on a private server we control. To categorize new activity accurately, the Service also maintains a working copy of the connected company’s recent transaction history, chart of accounts, and vendor list on that server; this working data is used only to perform bookkeeping for that company. We restrict access to the tokens, working data, and the server that holds them to authorized personnel. No method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so while we take reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How we share your data

We share information only as described here:

  • Intuit / QuickBooks Online — the source of the data, accessed through Intuit’s APIs under the permissions you grant.
  • AI provider (e.g., Anthropic) — as a service provider, only to process the categorization requests described under “AI processing.”
  • Authorized bookkeeping personnel — the people who operate the Service and review and approve entries on your behalf, which is a core function of the Service.
  • Legal and safety — where we are required to do so by applicable law, regulation, or legal process, or where necessary to protect against fraud, abuse, or security threats.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Data retention

Connection tokens are retained until you disconnect the Service from your QuickBooks Online company, until you ask us to delete them, or after a prolonged period of inactivity, whichever comes first. Working copies of your accounting data are retained only as long as needed to perform bookkeeping for the connected company and are deleted on request or when the connection ends.

Your choices and how to revoke access

You can review and revoke the Service’s access to your QuickBooks Online company at any time from within QuickBooks Online, under Settings → Apps (Connected Apps), or from your Intuit account’s connected-apps settings. To request deletion of any stored tokens or working data we hold about you, email matt@atmix.org and we will action the request promptly. Revoking access stops all future processing; it does not affect entries already recorded at your direction.

Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you (for example, under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation). To exercise any such right, email matt@atmix.org; we will respond within the time required by applicable law.
