Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atmix (“Atmix,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a project operated by Matt Cohen, handles information in connection with the Atmix Workspace Connector (the “Service”) and the website at atmix.org. The Service is a self-hosted connector built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP) that links a Google account you authorize to an AI assistant (such as Anthropic’s Claude), so that the assistant can carry out actions on your Google data that you explicitly request.

Questions about this policy: matt@atmix.org

Google user data we access

When you connect your Google account, the Service requests permission (via Google’s standard OAuth consent screen) to access the categories of data below. You see and approve the exact scopes on that screen, and the Service acts only on your account and only when you instruct the connected assistant to do so. We do not run background crawls of, or bulk-export, your data.

  • Gmail — read, search, draft, send, label, and organize messages in your mailbox, and read or adjust basic Gmail settings.
  • Google Drive — open, search, create, and modify files and folders in your Drive.
  • Google Contacts — read and update entries in your Contacts.
  • Google Calendar — read, create, and modify events on your calendars.
  • Google Sheets, Docs, and Slides — read and edit your spreadsheets, documents, and presentations.
  • Google Forms — read and edit your forms and read their responses.
  • Google Tasks — read and manage your tasks.
  • Google Apps Script — create, read, and run Apps Script projects that you authorize.
  • Google Chat — read and send messages in Chat spaces you participate in.

How we use your data

We use this access solely to carry out the specific tasks you direct through the connected assistant — for example, summarizing an email thread, drafting a reply you asked for, locating a file, updating a spreadsheet, or adding a calendar event. We do not access, process, or use your Google data for any purpose you have not requested.

Limited Use — Google API Services User Data Policy

Atmix’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular: we do not sell Google user data; we do not use or transfer it for serving advertisements; we do not use it to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models; and we do not allow humans to read your Google user data except (a) with your consent (for example, to troubleshoot a problem you have reported), (b) where necessary for security purposes or to comply with applicable law, or (c) where the data has been aggregated and anonymized and is used only for internal operations.

AI processing

To fulfill a request, the relevant portion of your Google data is transmitted to the AI provider that powers the assistant (for example, Anthropic) for processing in real time, and a result is returned to you. The AI provider processes this data as our service provider, under its own terms, only to perform the request — it does not use your Google data to train its models. Data sent for a request is not retained by the Service after the request completes, other than the connection tokens described below.

How we store and protect your data

To keep your account connected, we store the OAuth access and refresh tokens that Google issues, encrypted at rest, on a private server we control. We do not maintain a separate standing copy of your mailbox, files, contacts, or calendar; your content is fetched on demand to complete a request and is not warehoused. We restrict access to the tokens and the server that holds them. No method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so while we take reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How we share your data

We share information only as described here:

  • Google — the source of the data, accessed through Google’s APIs under the permissions you grant.
  • AI provider (e.g., Anthropic) — as a service provider, only to process the requests you make, as described under “AI processing.”
  • Legal and safety — where we are required to do so by applicable law, regulation, or legal process, or where necessary to protect against fraud, abuse, or security threats.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Data retention

Connection tokens are retained until you revoke the Service’s access, until you ask us to delete them, or after a prolonged period of inactivity, whichever comes first. Transient request data is not retained by the Service after the request completes.

Your choices and how to revoke access

You can review and revoke the Service’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any stored connection tokens or other information we hold about you, email matt@atmix.org and we will action the request promptly. Revoking access stops all future processing; it does not affect actions already completed at your direction.

Website visitors

atmix.org is a static website. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking technologies on it. Our host (GitHub Pages) may automatically log standard technical request data, such as IP address and browser user agent, for security and operational purposes.

Children

The Service and this website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you (for example, under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation). To exercise any such right, email matt@atmix.org; we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, we will update the “Last updated” date above and post the revised version at this URL. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
