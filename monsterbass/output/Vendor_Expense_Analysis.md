## SECTION 6: FLAGS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

### FLAG 1: REDUNDANT CONTENT/MEDIA CONTRACTORS ($218K/year)
**Severity: HIGH -- Potential savings $80-120K/year**

Six to seven separate content/media contractors is excessive for a $1.7M business:
- TrailBlaze Media ($5K/mo) + Dimension Studios ($5-8K/mo) + Shane Park Media ($3K/mo) + Jeremy Francis ($2.4K/mo) -- these four alone are $15-18K/month on content production.
- Creating Outdoors Consulting ($4.5K/mo) adds strategic creative on top.
- Michael Meisenheimer ($2.5K/mo) has an unclear role.

**Recommendation**: Consolidate to two contractors maximum -- one for video/content production, one for creative strategy. This could save $8-10K/month ($96-120K/year).

### FLAG 2: MARKETING SPEND AT 40% OF REVENUE
**Severity: HIGH -- Revenue does not justify this level**

Total marketing + creative = $677K on $1.7M revenue = 39.8%. For a mature subscription box/e-commerce business, 15-20% is the target. Even at 25% (aggressive), you would spend $426K -- saving $251K.

**Recommendation**: Set a hard marketing budget ceiling of 25% of trailing 3-month revenue. Cut underperforming channels first. Demand ROAS reporting from every agency/platform.

### FLAG 3: FEDEX SHIPPING COSTS ($239K)
**Severity: MEDIUM -- Potential savings $40-80K/year**

FedEx is being charged entirely to the Amex card (~$20K/month). For subscription box shipments, USPS Priority Mail or UPS Ground are typically 20-40% cheaper than FedEx for boxes under 5 lbs.

**Recommendation**: Negotiate volume rates with FedEx, or shift more volume to USPS (already in use at $78K). A 25% reduction in FedEx costs alone saves $60K.

### FLAG 4: YOTPO AT $35K/YEAR
**Severity: MEDIUM -- Potential savings $15-25K/year**

Yotpo spiked to $8K in April (likely an annual commitment or upgrade). At $35K/year for reviews and loyalty, this is expensive. Alternatives like Judge.me ($15/mo) for reviews + a simpler loyalty program could achieve 80% of the functionality at 90% less cost.

**Recommendation**: Evaluate whether Yotpo's loyalty + review combo is delivering measurable revenue lift. If not, downgrade or replace.

### FLAG 5: RETENTION TOOL OVERLAP (Retentio $24K + Klaviyo $18K + Live Recover $302)
**Severity: MEDIUM -- Potential savings $24K/year**

Retentio ($2K/mo), Klaviyo, and Live Recover all operate in the retention/email/SMS space. Klaviyo alone can handle email marketing, SMS, flows, and cart recovery.

**Recommendation**: Evaluate whether Retentio provides unique value above what Klaviyo flows deliver. If not, eliminate Retentio and save $24K/year.

### FLAG 6: DIMENSION STUDIOS RAMPING FAST (NEW VENDOR)
**Severity: WATCH -- $7-8K/month and growing**

Dimension Studios appeared in September at $645 and ramped to $7.8K by December. At current trajectory, this is a $94K/year vendor. Combined with the existing content contractors, this adds to an already bloated creative budget.

**Recommendation**: Clarify scope. If Dimension Studios is replacing other contractors, ensure those contracts are being wound down simultaneously.

### FLAG 7: TIKTOK ADS RAMPING WITHOUT VALIDATION
**Severity: WATCH -- $22.5K spent, accelerating**

TikTok went from $0 to $9.8K in December with no visible revenue lift (Oct-Nov were among the lowest revenue months). This may take time to build, but uncontrolled ramp without ROAS tracking is risky.

**Recommendation**: Cap TikTok at current levels until Q1 ROAS data is available.

### FLAG 8: SHOPIFY CAPITAL LOAN ($140K drawn, $62K repaid)
**Severity: NOTE**

A $140K Shopify Capital draw occurred in September. Repayments of $62K have been deducted from Shopify payouts through year-end. This is expensive capital (Shopify typically charges 10-17% factor rate). This inflated September cash inflows.

### FLAG 9: "ASK MY ACCOUNTANT" CATEGORY ($4,142)
**Severity: BOOKKEEPING ISSUE**

$4,142 in expenses are classified as "ask my accountant" -- these are uncategorized charges that need proper classification.

### FLAG 10: RICK PATRI -- EXECUTIVE PAY + AMEX + INTERCOMPANY ($810K gross flows)
**Severity: GOVERNANCE NOTE**

Rick Patri (likely the owner/CEO) has $810K in gross outflows: $40K executive pay, $265K in Amex card payments, and $100K to Maximus Outdoors (intercompany). The Amex card carries $239K in FedEx charges plus other operating expenses. While the Amex payments are not personal compensation, the $265K in card payments and $100K intercompany transfer need clear documentation for audit purposes.
