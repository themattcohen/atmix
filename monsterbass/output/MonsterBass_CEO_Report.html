Two-Book Accounting Structure (Not a Problem)

MonsterBass uses two QuickBooks books that serve different, complementary purposes. This is intentional and beneficial - not a red flag.

OUTDOOR PLAYGROUND (Primary)

Use for ALL financial reporting

  • 11,260 GL transactions
  • Reconciled bank accounts
  • Complete chart of accounts
  • P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow
  • Tax reporting ready
MONSTERBASS (Legacy)

Use for SKU cost data ONLY

  • 1,318 bills with product detail
  • Product names with UPCs
  • Unit costs per variant
  • Historical vendor-product mapping
  • NOT for financial statements

Key Point: The legacy books provide SKU-level detail that does NOT exist in the primary books. When combined with Shopify sales data, this enables product-level margin analysis. No consolidation is needed - they are different data types for different purposes.
