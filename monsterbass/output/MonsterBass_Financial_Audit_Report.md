#### Issue #1: Cash Runway Crisis

**Current Position:**
- Cash: $100,328 (December 2025)
- Monthly burn: ~$100,000
- Runway: **Less than 1 month**

**Contributing Factors:**
- Shopify Capital loan ($140K in Sept 2025) indicates cash desperation
- Daily automatic repayments (~$15-20K/month) now consuming cash flow
- Credit card debt used as working capital

**Shopify Capital Note:** Balance declined from $138K to $78K in 3 months, implying ~$60K paid back. This rapid repayment is draining operating cash.
