## 1. Executive Summary

**The opportunity**: Mobile phlebotomy is a $1.67 billion global market growing at 7.3% CAGR, and Denver's competitive landscape is wide open. No local provider dominates. The top organic search results for "mobile phlebotomy Denver" are a mix of directories (Yelp), thin national landing pages, and small solo operators with Wix sites. Nobody owns this market.

**USMM LLC's advantage**: US Mobile Medics already ranks ~4th for "mobile phlebotomy Denver." U.S. Mobile IV has 546 Google reviews at a perfect 5.0, a BBB A- rating, and 8 local partnerships. Combined, these assets represent a trust surplus that no competitor in this space can match. The phlebotomy service exists -- it just needs its own brand, its own identity, and its own growth engine.

**The play**: Launch **U.S. Mobile Labs** as a purpose-built phlebotomy sub-brand. Retire the underperforming US Mobile Medics brand (D+ audit grade). Transfer the phlebotomy service, pricing transparency, and clinical positioning into a new entity that inherits trust from U.S. Mobile IV while carving out an independent identity in a category with almost zero local competition.

**The prize**: The Denver metro has 400,000+ residents aged 65+, a $13.9 billion hospital market, 9+ concierge medicine practices, and dozens of home health agencies -- all of whom need mobile phlebotomy and currently have limited options. With transparent pricing, a modern digital presence, and aggressive review collection, U.S. Mobile Labs can realistically become the #1 ranked mobile phlebotomy provider in Denver within 12 months.

**Key metrics to hit**:
- Rank #1 for "mobile phlebotomy Denver" within 6-9 months
- 50+ Google reviews within 6 months
- 6-8 consumer draws per day + 2-3 B2B contracts by month 6
- Revenue run rate of $15,000-$20,000/month by month 6

### 2.2 Growth Drivers

**Post-COVID consumer behavior shift**: 47% of consumers now prefer home testing over clinic visits for routine screenings. Telehealth stabilized at 4.5% of all healthcare visits -- physicians ordering labs via telehealth need somewhere for patients to get blood drawn. Mobile phlebotomy fills that gap.

**Aging population**: Americans aged 65+ will grow from 58M today to 71M by 2030 (+23%). Colorado has the 3rd fastest-aging population in the U.S. (+58.1% senior growth 2013-2023). 87% of seniors want to age in place. Denver metro has 400,000+ residents aged 65+.

**Chronic disease monitoring**: Diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, and kidney disease all require regular blood work. These patients are the highest-frequency users of phlebotomy services and are the most inconvenienced by repeated lab visits.

**Direct-to-consumer testing boom**: The DTC lab testing market is $3.75B and growing at 9% CAGR. Companies like Everlywell and LetsGetChecked have normalized at-home specimen collection. Mobile phlebotomy serves the segment that needs venous draws (not finger-prick kits).

| Denver metro 65+ population | 400,000+ |

| Home health agencies | Dozens (BAYADA, Denver HHC, HealthONE, etc.) |

### 2.4 Addressable Market Estimate

Based on the convergence of consumer preference data, the routine nature of most outpatient blood draws, and the accessibility needs of elderly/homebound populations, an estimated **15-25% of routine outpatient blood draws are addressable by mobile phlebotomy services**.

Colorado performs an estimated 125-250 million lab tests annually (1.8% of national volume). If even 1% of these shift to mobile collection, that represents 1.25-2.5 million draws -- far exceeding the capacity of any single local provider.

### 4.3 Decision Drivers by Segment

**What makes someone choose mobile over going to the lab?**

1. **Physical limitation** (can't easily leave home) -- seniors, disabled, post-surgical

3. **Experience expectation** (paying for concierge-level care) -- DPC patients, premium wellness
4. **Coordination burden** (organizing group logistics is painful) -- corporate HR, facility managers
5. **Emotional comfort** (anxiety, needle phobia, pediatric trauma) -- anxious patients, parents with children

## 5. Brand Architecture Decision

### 5.1 Options Evaluated

| Option | Description | Trust Transfer | SEO Benefit | Independence | Recommendation |
|--------|-------------|---------------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| **A: Sub-brand** | "U.S. Mobile Labs" -- same prefix, different service word | Full -- instantly recognized as family | Cross-linking, shared domain authority | Moderate | RECOMMENDED |
| B: Endorsed | "NewBrand -- powered by U.S. Mobile IV" | Partial -- requires explanation | Some link equity transfer | High | Viable alternative |
| C: Sibling | Shared visual DNA, separate name | Minimal -- must build from zero | Separate domain, no transfer | Full | Suboptimal |
| D: Independent | No connection to parent | None | None | Full | Wrong for this situation |

### 5.2 Recommendation: Sub-Brand (Option A)

**"U.S. Mobile Labs"** -- same "U.S. Mobile" family prefix, with "Labs" clearly communicating the clinical/blood work service category.

**Rationale**:

1. **Trust transfer is critical in healthcare.** The 546 reviews at 5.0 stars represent a trust surplus. Non-consumption of mobile phlebotomy exists because people don't trust unknown mobile healthcare providers. A recognized family name collapses that trust barrier.

2. **The tribe already exists.** U.S. Mobile IV's customers have already opted into "healthcare comes to me." They have given permission to be contacted about blood draws. This is the warmest possible audience.

3. **SEO authority transfers.** Cross-linking between usmobileiv.com and the new domain creates link equity. The shared "U.S. Mobile" brand signals to Google that these are related, authoritative entities.

4. **Brand recognition compounds.** "U.S. Mobile IV" + "U.S. Mobile Labs" = "U.S. Mobile" becomes a platform brand. Future extensions (U.S. Mobile Wellness, U.S. Mobile Diagnostics) become natural.

5. **Competitive moat.** No other Denver mobile phlebotomy provider has a trust asset of this magnitude. ABOLabs has 50+ reviews; Getlabs has VC funding. USMM has 546 reviews at a perfect 5.0 + BBB A- + 8 partnerships. This cannot be replicated.

## 7. Visual Identity Direction

### 7.1 Design Principles

The visual identity must accomplish three things simultaneously:
1. **Signal clinical competence** -- this is healthcare, not a spa
2. **Feel approachable and warm** -- this is your home, not a lab waiting room
3. **Read as family with U.S. Mobile IV** without being identical

### 7.2 Color Palette

U.S. Mobile IV uses teal (#44B7BC). The new brand must be distinct but harmonious -- sharing blue undertones while establishing its own identity.

| Role | Color | Hex | WCAG on White | Usage |
|------|-------|-----|---------------|-------|
| **Primary** | Deep Navy | #1B4D6E | 8.2:1 (AAA) | Headers, primary text, hero backgrounds |
| **Secondary** | Warm Coral | #E07A5F | 3.4:1 (AA large text) | CTAs, accents, highlights, buttons |
| **Tertiary** | Sage Green | #5B8C6F | 4.1:1 (AA large text) | Trust badges, health/vitality signals |
| **Accent** | Soft Gold | #D4A574 | 2.8:1 (decorative only) | Premium indicators, subtle highlights |
| **Background** | Off-White | #F7F5F2 | -- | Section backgrounds |
| **Dark** | Deep Blue-Black | #0A1F2E | 16.5:1 (AAA) | Dark sections, hero backgrounds |
| **Text** | Charcoal | #2D2D2D | 14.5:1 (AAA) | Body text |

**Why this palette works**:
- Navy is the most trusted color in healthcare after white
- Coral humanizes the clinical navy -- warm, approachable, action-oriented
- Sage signals health and vitality without being "medical green"
- The palette is distinct from U.S. Mobile IV's teal but shares blue undertones, creating family cohesion
- All primary/text colors exceed WCAG AA contrast requirements (fixing the accessibility failure from both existing sites)

### 7.3 Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Usage |
|------|------|--------|-------|
| **Headlines** | Plus Jakarta Sans | 600-700 | H1-H3, hero text, section titles |
| **Body** | Inter | 400-500 | Paragraphs, descriptions, captions |
| **Accent** | Inter | 600 | CTAs, navigation, badges, pricing |

**Why these fonts**:
- Plus Jakarta Sans: Geometric, clean, modern but professional. Reads as "tech-forward healthcare" not "hospital."
- Inter: The most readable sans-serif on screens. Designed specifically for UI. Familiar but not generic.
- Both are Google Fonts (free, fast loading, no licensing issues).

### 7.4 Tone of Voice

| Attribute | What It Sounds Like | What It Does NOT Sound Like |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Confident** | "We'll be there at 8am sharp." | "We hope to arrive around 8." |
| **Warm** | "We make lab day easy." | "Optimizing your specimen collection experience." |
| **Direct** | "Book online. We come to you. Done." | "Explore our comprehensive mobile phlebotomy service offerings." |
| **Clinical when needed** | "Our phlebotomists are nationally certified." | "Our team is super experienced!" |
| **Transparent** | "A routine draw is $99. That's it." | "Contact us for a personalized quote." |

**Voice archetype**: A competent, friendly nurse. Not a corporation. Not a startup. A person you'd trust with a needle in your arm.

### 7.5 Logo Direction

**Concept**: A simplified blood drop integrated with a location pin or home icon. Encodes "blood draw" + "we come to you" in one mark.

**"U.S. Mobile Labs"** wordmark in Plus Jakarta Sans 700 weight, with "U.S. Mobile" in navy and "Labs" in coral as a visual connector to the brand palette.

### 8.2 Pricing Strategy

**Philosophy**: Transparent, competitive, no hidden fees. All prices include travel within Denver metro (25-mile radius).

| Tier | Price | What's Included | Target Position |
|------|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Standard Draw** | $99 | Single visit, up to 4 tubes, specimen courier to lab | Below Speedy Sticks ($150), above Getlabs ($35), competitive with Quest Mobile ($79) |
| **Comprehensive Panel** | $149 | Full panel draw + same-day/weekend availability | Premium convenience |
| **Executive Screening** | $199 | Extensive panel + specialty markers + priority scheduling | Premium tier |
| **Corporate (per person)** | $65 | On-site draw, groups of 10+ | Volume pricing |
| **Concierge Retainer** | $500-800/mo | Unlimited draws for practice's patient panel | B2B recurring |
| **Travel surcharge** | $25 | Beyond 25-mile radius | Cost recovery |
| **After-hours/weekend** | +$25 | Before 7am, after 7pm, Sundays | Demand premium |

**Why $99 for the standard draw**: 

- At $99, we are priced above the budget tier (Getlabs at $35, Kryssys at $25-50) but below the premium tier (Speedy Sticks at $150, US Mobile Medics packages at $175+).
- $99 signals quality without being exclusionary. It's a number that busy professionals won't blink at but also won't scare away seniors on fixed incomes.
- $99 delivers a 59% gross margin (cost per visit: ~$35-41), which is sustainable and allows for marketing investment.
- $99 is memorable, transparent, and easy to communicate. "Ninety-nine dollars, all-in."

## 12. Regulatory & Compliance

### 12.1 Compliance Checklist

**Phlebotomy Certification**
- [ ] Colorado does NOT require state phlebotomy licensure
- [ ] National certification recommended for credibility and contracts
  - ASCP PBT (preferred by hospitals/labs)
  - NHA CPT (common for mobile phlebotomists)
  - AMT (alternative)
- [ ] Certification cost: $125-$145 per phlebotomist
- [ ] Training: 4-12 week program + clinical hours

**CLIA Compliance**
- [ ] Certificate of Waiver (~$200/2 years) if performing any point-of-care testing
- [ ] For specimen collection only (no on-site testing): CLIA waiver may not be required
- [ ] For Medicare billing: must work under a CLIA-certified lab's license or enroll own entity

**HIPAA**
- [ ] Privacy policies and procedures documented
- [ ] Patient consent forms for specimen collection
- [ ] Secure storage/transmission of PHI (encrypted devices, secure portals)
- [ ] Business Associate Agreements with labs, couriers, tech vendors
- [ ] Staff HIPAA training with documentation
- [ ] Breach notification procedures
- [ ] **Booking system must be HIPAA-compliant** (fix the `isHipaa: false` issue from Acuity)

**Specimen Transport (DOT/OSHA)**
- [ ] Triple Packaging System compliance
- [ ] DOT training for anyone handling/packing infectious substances
- [ ] Temperature-controlled transport (2-8C for most specimens)
- [ ] Spill kit in vehicle
- [ ] Biohazard labeling on all containers

**Business Licensing**
- [ ] Colorado business entity registration (Secretary of State) -- USMM LLC already exists
- [ ] DBA filing for "U.S. Mobile Labs" under USMM LLC
- [ ] Denver business license (if not already held)
- [ ] EIN (existing or new for DBA)

**Insurance**
- [ ] Professional liability (malpractice): $500-$3,000/year
- [ ] General liability: standard business coverage
- [ ] Commercial auto insurance (if using personal vehicle)
- [ ] Workers' compensation (if hiring employees)

**Medical Waste**
- [ ] Sharps disposal contract with licensed vendor
- [ ] OSHA Bloodborne Pathogen Standard compliance
- [ ] Vehicle sharps container protocols

### 12.2 Non-Negotiable Day-One Requirements

These must be in place before the first patient draw:

1. HIPAA-compliant booking system (Acuity with HIPAA enabled, Jane App, or similar)
2. Nationally certified phlebotomist(s)
3. Professional liability insurance
4. DOT-compliant specimen transport containers
5. Sharps disposal contract
6. Patient consent forms
7. Lab partnership (for specimen processing)
