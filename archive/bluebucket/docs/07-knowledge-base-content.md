## Services Offered

### House Cleaning (Residential)

#### Standard Cleaning
Our standard cleaning service includes:

**Kitchen**
- Wipe down countertops and backsplash
- Clean exterior of appliances (stove, microwave, refrigerator)
- Clean sink and polish fixtures
- Wipe down cabinet fronts
- Clean inside microwave
- Empty trash and replace liner
- Sweep and mop floors

**Bathrooms**
- Clean and sanitize toilet (inside and out)
- Clean shower/tub and glass doors
- Clean sink and polish fixtures
- Wipe down counters
- Clean mirrors
- Wipe down cabinet fronts
- Empty trash
- Sweep and mop floors

**Bedrooms & Living Areas**
- Dust all accessible surfaces
- Dust ceiling fans and light fixtures (within reach)
- Dust blinds and window sills
- Vacuum carpets and rugs
- Vacuum/mop hard floors
- Make beds (with linens in place)
- Empty trash cans
- Dust furniture and decor

**General**
- Dust baseboards (monthly rotation)
- Dust door frames
- Wipe light switches and door handles
- Spot clean mirrors and glass
- Organize clutter into neat piles (not put away unless requested)

#### Deep Cleaning
Everything in Standard Cleaning PLUS:

**Additional Deep Clean Services**
- Inside all cabinets and drawers (emptied by client or additional charge)
- Inside refrigerator and freezer
- Inside oven and stove top (heavy degreasing)
- Baseboards throughout (detailed cleaning)
- All ceiling fans and light fixtures
- Window sills and tracks (detailed)
- Behind and under furniture (accessible areas)
- Inside closets (surface cleaning)
- Walls - spot cleaning marks and scuffs
- Vents and registers
- Detailed door cleaning (both sides)

#### Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning
Comprehensive cleaning for empty or transitioning properties:

- All Deep Cleaning services
- Inside all cabinets, closets, and drawers
- Inside all appliances
- Detailed baseboard cleaning
- Light fixture detailed cleaning
- Window cleaning (interior)
- Garage sweep (if applicable)
- Detailed attention to all hardware and fixtures

## Objection Handling Responses

### Price Objections

**"That's too expensive"**
"I understand price is a consideration - it's important to get good value. What many of our customers find is that when they add up their own time, the cost of supplies, and the hassle, professional cleaning actually saves money. Plus, with our bi-weekly service, you get a 10% discount on every visit, which brings it to [calculated price]. And we have a 100% satisfaction guarantee - if you're ever not happy, we'll make it right at no extra charge."

**"I can clean myself for free"**
"That's absolutely true, and some people prefer to do it themselves. What our customers tell us is that they value getting their weekends back, having a consistently clean home, and the peace of mind that comes with professional attention to detail. For most people, the 3-4 hours we save them every other week is worth it. Would you like to try us for just one cleaning to see if it makes a difference?"

**"Other companies are cheaper"**
"I appreciate you sharing that - price definitely matters. A few things to consider when comparing: we're fully bonded and insured, our team passes background checks, we use eco-friendly products, and we offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. Many customers who've switched to us from lower-priced services say the consistency and peace of mind is worth the difference. Would you like to try us for a single cleaning to see the quality difference?"

### Timing Objections

**"I need to think about it"**
"Absolutely, take your time - this is a decision worth thinking through. Quick question though: is getting professional cleaning something you're planning to do in the next month or so, or is it just not on your radar right now? ... If it's something you're considering, I'd be happy to hold a time slot for you while you decide - we do book up quickly, especially for [day of week] appointments."

**"Call me back later"**
"Of course, I can do that. When would be a better time - later today, or tomorrow morning? ... Perfect, I'll call you back then. In the meantime, would it be helpful if I texted you a summary of the quote so you have it handy?"

**"Not right now"**
"No problem at all. Just so I can note it in your file - is it more of a timing thing, or is professional cleaning not something you're interested in? ... Got it. Well, when [timing changes], we'd love to help. Can I send you our contact info so you have it when you're ready?"

### Trust Objections

**"I want to talk to a real person"**
"Completely understand, and I appreciate you being upfront. I can connect you with someone from our team right now if you'd prefer. Or, if you'd like, I can gather a few quick details first so they're fully prepared when they call - that way you don't have to repeat yourself. Which would you prefer?"

**"I'm uncomfortable with strangers in my home"**
"That's a very valid concern - we hear that from many first-time customers. Here's how we address it: every team member passes a thorough background check before they start. They arrive in uniform with company ID, in a marked company vehicle. We're fully bonded and insured, so you're protected. And many of our clients start with being home for the first cleaning just to meet the team and see how we work. Does that help put your mind at ease?"

**"How do I know you're legitimate?"**
"Great question - you should absolutely verify that. We're a licensed business in Colorado, fully bonded and insured with $[amount] in coverage. You can find our reviews on Google, Yelp, and Angi - we have [X] stars with over [X] reviews. And our website has been around since [year]. Would you like me to send you links to verify?"

### Competition Objections
